# Feature: Community
- **Assignee:** Reehan
- **Status:** in-progress
- **One-liner:** Hybrid vouching platform: borrowers post an ask and choose what strangers may see; anyone can vouch as someone who knows them (KNOWN) or as a stranger who read their verified summary (EVIDENCE, weighted by the voucher's track record and capped); vouchers stand by or step back after a setback; live on `/api/v1/community` when the backend answers (vouches, previews, stand-by and what strangers see all go to the server), demo data when it does not, with a badge saying which
