# Demo

**Why this file exists:** the demo is what gets judged. A written script and a fallback plan mean we can still present if the network, API or deploy fails.

## Script
Start the app with `make dev` and open `http://127.0.0.1:5173`. The dark presenter bar at the bottom shows the next step and the button for it. All figures are illustrative seed data.

0. Community (`/community`, as Meena): open Lakshmi (in your circle), vouch as a neighbour of 9 years and hold; her score moves as the preview said. Open Farida (a stranger): switch to "I read their file", tick the box, and see the smaller weight and bigger reward for backing a thin file. Open Suresh: his setback is posted; press Stand by and the lender line reads "4 of 4 stood by". "My ask" shows Lakshmi choosing what strangers may see.
1. Landing: "Start the guided story" opens sign-in; "Run a live assessment" opens the Assessment desk. Every app screen has the same top bar for Guided story, Community and Assessment desk (the dot is green when the backend answers). The hero panel cycles three cases (hover pauses it). Lakshmi has no bureau file and the one-number lender declines her; Vouch shows four separate signals. Suresh's score fell 77 points after a shock while his endorsements held. Account 7731's twelve ring endorsements score 8, not 55. Scroll: "The problem" pins while you scroll through three pain points (19% of 150,000 with no score; 35 on-time months and one late month after a hospital bill costing 50-80 CIBIL points, a lender's estimate; 45% never checked their score). "How it works" plays its four steps: borrower and peers, Vouch's checks, the lender's three signals, the lender decides. Then "Does it work?": test 01 replays real Prosper loans (opens on scores that fell 20+: 21.5% vs 30.5% default), test 02 splits 2,289 simulated shocked borrowers by vouch (21.5% / 34.7% / 47.9%), test 03 is a vouch-strength slider. Then the research cards (each linked to its source, with what it does not show) set up the problem; the counter-evidence card is why vouches are weighted, not counted. Below, tab 01 lets you toggle endorsers and watch diminishing returns; tab 02 opens the stress test on her worst month. "Business" shows who pays whom: switch between institution-owned and platform; borrowers never pay Vouch. "Three ways in" at the bottom explains each part before you open it.
2. Sign-in (`/login`): pick Lakshmi (`0000 0000 4821`), any six-digit code, allow DigiLocker. The nullifier is revealed with its identity glyph: credibility 0, uncertainty 1.00.
3. Borrower: score -1.00 with a wide hatched band, limit 0 INR. Click "Ask someone who knows your work" and send to Meena.
4. Voucher (Meena): open the request. The meter re-splits from 0.93 to 0.70 per vouch; exposure is -0.35 if Lakshmi defaults, +0.10 if she repays; Lakshmi goes from 0 INR to 6,000 INR. Hold to vouch.
5. Borrower: limit 6,000 INR; the path table shows two anchor paths and an absorbed loop. Optionally link statements to watch the band narrow. Apply for 5,000 INR.
6. Lender: open Lakshmi's file. Hover a path row to light it in the graph; DSCR and both axes sit beside it. Approve.
7. Presenter bar: "Advance 30 days", then "Lakshmi repays" (credibility minted, Meena earns yield) or "Lakshmi defaults" (graph ripples: Lakshmi -1.00, Meena -0.35, three ancestors -0.25 each, Sahyog marked "hop 3, untouched").
8. "Regional shock": three Gadag farmers default at once, the breaker trips at above 30%, the third slash is queued. Click "Restructure and resume".
9. Anti-churn: "Reset", then sign in as Vinod (`0000 0000 0007`). The same nullifier resolves to his DEFAULTED record.
10. Live backend (optional; start it with `cd backend && python main.py serve --port 8000`, the green dot on "Assess" turns on): open `/app/assess`. It opens on Lakshmi asking for 1,50,000 INR over 12 months; Run assessment shows bureau (no file, unknown not bad), Vouch Score 63.5 from Ramesh and Anand, affordability (8,945 INR a month to spare) and evidence apart, and offers a restructured plan. Move the what-if sliders: with nothing moved, the scenario matches the assessment. Below, the predictive model gives its own view (decision, default probability, reasons, missing evidence); then approve or decline. An approval puts the loan in the lender console's live book, where Repay records a payment with its 1% platform fee and Default shows the cascade. The community (`/community`) also runs live: the badge in its header says "live backend".

## Fallback plan
- No network is needed: fonts are self-hosted and the protocol runs in the browser (`frontend/src/mocks/`).
- Jump straight to any scene with `?jump=<step>`, for example `http://127.0.0.1:5173/app/lender/p_lakshmi?jump=approve`. Steps: `request`, `vouch`, `apply`, `approve`, `advance`, `outcome`, `settled`, `defaulted`.
- If the dev server fails, run `npm run build` then `npm run preview` in `frontend/` (serves `dist/` on port 4173).
- Backend on another machine: start the frontend with `VITE_API_BASE_URL=http://<host>:8000`. If the backend is down, every step still runs: the Assessment desk shows recorded runs (1,50,000 INR over 12 months per applicant) and the community uses demo data, each with a "demo data" badge; nothing shows an error. A stale `backend/data/store.json` from the old ALICE and BOB seed is replaced automatically on start.
