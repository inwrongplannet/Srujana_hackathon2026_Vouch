# Vouch — How the System Works

## Headline

**Trust is useful evidence. But it needs to be earned.**

**Vouch is a complementary layer that combines accountable peer endorsements with consent-based financial evidence.**

It helps lenders see beyond a credit score without replacing the credit bureau or the lender's judgment.

## The Vouch Flow

### 01 — A borrower applies
The borrower requests a loan and, with consent, provides available financial evidence.

### 02 — People vouch for them
People who know the borrower put their own platform reputation behind their endorsement. Credibility matters more than endorsement count.

### 03 — Vouch evaluates the evidence
Peer endorsements are checked for credibility and manipulation. Financial evidence and existing obligations are assessed separately to understand affordability.

### 04 — The lender sees the full picture
Bureau information, Vouch Score, affordability, supporting evidence, and uncertainties are presented separately. The lender makes the final decision.

## Suggested Visual Layout

Use three visual zones flowing from left to right:

**Borrower + Trusted Peers**
- Borrower: loan request
- Trusted peers: accountable endorsements

→ **Vouch Assessment**
- Credibility and manipulation checks
- Financial evidence: income, obligations, and cash flow

→ **Lender View**
Show three separate signals:
- Bureau profile
- Vouch Score
- Affordability

Each signal should have supporting evidence and limitations visible. End with: **The lender decides.**

## Closing Line

**More context. Clearer reasons. A human decision.**

## Design Notes

- Keep the page visual-first, with minimal text and a clear flow diagram.
- Do not include scoring formulas, research statistics, or pricing on this page; those belong in their own sections.
- The purpose is for a judge to understand the mechanism in about five seconds.
- Make clear that Vouch does not simply add a social score to CIBIL. It checks endorsement credibility, evaluates affordability independently, and gives the lender an explainable view of the evidence.
